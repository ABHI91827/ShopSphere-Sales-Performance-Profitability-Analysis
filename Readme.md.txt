ShopSphere Sales Performance & Profitability Analysis
An end-to-end Business Intelligence project that identifies the root causes behind declining revenue and profit using SQL Server, Power BI, and Excel.
________________________________________
📌 Project Overview
ShopSphere, a retail company, experienced a slight decline in revenue and profit between 2024 and 2025, despite maintaining stable customer activity and order volume. The objective of this project was to analyze business performance, identify the root causes of the decline, and provide data-driven recommendations to improve profitability.
This project follows a real-world analytics workflow, including business understanding, data validation, SQL analysis, dashboard development, and executive reporting.
________________________________________
🎯 Business Problem
Although customer activity remained stable, ShopSphere observed a decline in both revenue and profit during 2025. Management wanted to understand:
•	Why is revenue decreasing? 
•	Which product categories are underperforming? 
•	Is the decline caused by fewer customers, lower order values, higher discounts, or increasing costs? 
•	What actions can improve future business performance? 
________________________________________
🎯 Project Objectives
•	Analyze sales performance across products, regions, and customer segments. 
•	Identify the factors contributing to declining revenue and profit. 
•	Evaluate the impact of Average Order Value (AOV), pricing, discounts, and product categories. 
•	Build an interactive Power BI dashboard for business decision-making. 
•	Provide actionable recommendations based on data insights.

Root Cause Analysis
The investigation produced the following findings.
Supported Findings
•	Average Order Value decreased. 
•	Beauty, Home & Kitchen, and Electronics experienced lower revenue. 
•	Beauty and Electronics recorded a slight decline in profit margin. 
Rejected Findings
•	Customer loss 
•	Order volume decline 
•	Rising operational costs 
•	Higher discounts 
________________________________________
Key Insights
•	Revenue decreased from ₹428.38M to ₹427.14M. 
•	Profit decreased from ₹166.59M to ₹166.21M. 
•	Customer count remained stable. 
•	Order volume remained stable. 
•	Average Order Value declined. 
•	Beauty remained the highest revenue contributor despite declining sales. 
•	Fashion and Sports showed positive growth. 
•	Stable costs confirmed that profitability declined primarily because of lower revenue rather than increasing expenses.
