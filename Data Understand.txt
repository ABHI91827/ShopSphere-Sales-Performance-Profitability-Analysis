Data Understanding
1. Purpose

The purpose of this document is to provide an understanding of the ShopSphere sales dataset used for the Sales Performance & Profitability Analysis project.

It describes the data sources, table structures, relationships, business metrics, and assumptions used throughout the analysis. This document serves as a reference for analysts, developers, and business stakeholders.

2. Dataset Overview

The dataset contains transactional sales data for ShopSphere, a multi-category e-commerce retailer. It captures customer purchases, product information, promotions, shipping details, and calendar dimensions.

The data is organized using a Star Schema, where a central fact table stores transactional data and multiple dimension tables provide descriptive information.

4. Data Model

The project follows a Star Schema.

Fact Table
Table Name	Description
Fact_Sales	Stores every sales transaction for each product purchased in an order.
Dimension Tables
Table Name	Description
Dim_Date	Calendar information such as day, month, quarter, and year.
Dim_Product	Product attributes including category and brand.
Dim_Customer	Customer demographic information.
Dim_Promotion	Promotion and discount details.
Dim_Shipping	Shipping-related information.

5. Data Granularity

The Fact_Sales table is stored at the Order Line Level.

Each row represents one product purchased within a customer order.

Example:

Order ID	Product	Quantity
ORD1001	Shampoo	2
ORD1001	Hair Dryer	1

The same order can contain multiple rows if multiple products were purchased.

8. Business Metrics

The following KPIs are used throughout the project.

Metric	                        Formula	                                 Description
Revenue	                        SUM(Revenue)	                         Total sales generated.
Profit	                        SUM(Profit)	                         Total business profit.
Orders	                        DISTINCTCOUNT(Order_ID)	                 Total unique customer orders.
Customers	                DISTINCTCOUNT(Customer_ID)	         Total unique customers.
Quantity Sold	                SUM(Quantity)	                         Total units sold.
Average Order Value (AOV)	Revenue ÷ Orders	                 Average revenue per order.
Average Selling Price (ASP)	Revenue ÷ Quantity	                 Average selling price per unit.
Discount	                SUM(Discount)	                         Total discounts provided.
Profit Margin %	                (Profit ÷ Revenue) × 100	         Percentage of revenue retained as profit.
Cost %	                        (Cost ÷ Revenue) × 100	                 Percentage of revenue spent on product cost.

9. Business Rules

The following business rules are assumed during analysis:

Each Order_ID is unique at the order level.
Each product belongs to only one category.
Revenue values are recorded after discounts are applied.
Profit is calculated as Revenue minus Cost Price.
Every sales transaction must be linked to a valid customer, product, and date.
All monetary values are recorded in Indian Rupees (₹).

10. Data Assumptions

The following assumptions were made:

The dataset is complete for the analysis period.
Customer IDs uniquely identify customers.
Product categories remain unchanged during the analysis period.
Date dimension contains all required calendar dates.
No manual adjustments were made outside the recorded transactions.

11. Data Limitations

The dataset has the following limitations:

Return and refund transactions are not available.
Inventory stock levels are not included.
Marketing campaign performance is limited to promotion information.
Customer satisfaction and product review data are unavailable.
Supplier details are not included, limiting procurement analysis.

12. Summary

The ShopSphere dataset follows a well-structured star schema with one central fact table and five supporting dimension tables. The data is stored at the order-line level, enabling detailed analysis across products, customers, categories, regions, promotions, and time. This structure supports efficient SQL querying, Power BI reporting, and business performance analysis.
