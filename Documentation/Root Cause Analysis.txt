Root Cause Analysis

Business Question:

Why did revenue and profit decline despite stable customer activity?


Investigation 1
Did customer activity decline?

Finding:

Orders remained stable.
Customers remained stable.

Conclusion:

Rejected.
Customer activity was not responsible.


Investigation 2
Did Average Order Value decrease?

Finding:

AOV decreased from

₹8,568.95

to

₹8,541.43

Conclusion:

Supported.
Customers spent slightly less per transaction.

Investigation 3
Were discounts responsible?

Finding:

Discount amount decreased.

Conclusion:

Rejected.
Discounts did not drive the decline.

Investigation 6:
Did costs increase?

Finding:

Total costs decreased slightly.
Cost percentage increased only marginally because revenue declined faster than costs.

Conclusion:

Cost was not a major driver of lower profitability.
Overall Root Cause
Revenue declined primarily because:
Average Order Value decreased.
Beauty, Home & Kitchen, and Electronics underperformed.

Profit declined because:

Revenue decreased.
Profit margins weakened in Beauty and Electronics.

No evidence suggested that customer loss, order volume, discounts, or rising costs were the primary causes.
