Business Requirements Document (BRD)

Project Name

ShopSphere Sales Performance & Profitability Analysis

1. Project Overview
Background

ShopSphere is a multi-category e-commerce retailer operating across Beauty, Electronics, Fashion, Home & Kitchen, and Sports categories.

During the annual business review, leadership observed that although customer acquisition and order volume remained relatively stable between 2024 and 2025, the company experienced a decline in both revenue and profitability.

Since the customer base has not significantly changed, management believes the decline is driven by operational or commercial factors rather than customer demand.

The executive team requires a detailed data-driven analysis to identify the underlying causes and recommend strategies to improve business performance.

2. Business Problem

Business reports indicate the following trends between 2024 and 2025:

KPI	Observation
Revenue	Decreased
Profit	Decreased
Orders	Stable
Customers	Stable

Although transaction volume has remained consistent, overall financial performance has deteriorated.


Management requires answers to the following questions:

Why did revenue decline?
Why did profit decline?
Which categories are underperforming?
Which products contribute most to the decline?
Is pricing strategy affecting profitability?
Are discounts reducing margins?
Are certain regions performing poorly?
Which KPIs require immediate attention?


3. Business Objectives

The primary objectives of this project are:

Measure overall sales performance.
Measure profitability across business dimensions.
Compare yearly business performance.
Identify declining product categories.
Identify low-performing products.
Evaluate discount effectiveness.
Analyze customer purchasing behavior.
Discover root causes behind declining revenue and profit.
Provide actionable business recommendations.
Develop an executive Power BI dashboard.
